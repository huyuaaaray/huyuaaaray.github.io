Selected Projects

Clinical LLM Evaluation and Model Selection for Readmission Review

Apr 2026 – Present
  • Led a head-to-head evaluation of Gemma 4 31B and MedGemma for patient-level readmission review across 30K–80K-token contexts, validating outputs against clinician review.
  • Identified a key safety failure mode, where the domain-tuned model inferred answers unsupported by the record while Gemma 4 abstained with evidence-specific rationales.
  • Built an LLM-as-a-Judge framework (Llama 3.3-70B) with rubric-based JSON verdicts and an HTML report that routes flagged patients to clinician review.
  • Benchmarked long-context inference on NVIDIA B200 GPUs (FP16 vs. FP8, tensor parallelism) and traced a throughput ceiling to vLLM's KV-cache handling of sliding-window attention.
